# Iron Log

A workout log that runs in the browser and installs to your phone's home screen.

- Log weight and reps, reps only, time, or done/not done, with optional left and right sides
- Templates for each workout day
- Easier and harder alternates you can swap in mid-workout; the app remembers your choice
- How-to notes, links, photos and videos for every exercise
- Works offline

**Your data stays on your phone.** Nothing is sent to a server. Use Settings > Backup to save a copy.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Iron Log from the new icon.

## Updating (maintainer)
Edit the files, then bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` and `APP_VERSION` in `index.html`. Installed copies pick up the new version the next time they open.
